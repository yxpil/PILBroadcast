中文

感谢您使用 PiLPublisher（以下简称「本软件」）。我们非常重视您的隐私，本策略将说明本软件如何处理您的数据。

1. 数据收集

本软件不收集、不存储、不上传您的任何个人数据或文件。

  • 本软件不会向任何远程服务器发送数据。
  • 本软件不会使用任何分析、统计或追踪工具。
  • 本软件不会读取您设备中未被您主动添加为共享文件夹的文件。
  • 您设置的访问密码仅存储在您的本地设备中，我们无法获取。

2. 文件共享

本软件的核心功能是在您的局域网内启动一个 HTTP 文件服务器（默认端口 9726），供局域网内的其他设备访问您选择的共享文件夹。

  • 您添加的共享文件夹及其中文件仅在您的局域网内可访问。
  • 互联网上的外部设备无法直接访问您的共享内容。
  • 您可以通过设置密码来限制访问。

3. 您的责任

本软件是一款工具，您应对自己的使用行为负责。

  • 请勿使用本软件共享侵犯他人权利（包括但不限于版权、隐私权、肖像权）的内容。
  • 请勿使用本软件传播违法、色情、暴力、仇恨言论或其他不良信息。
  • 如因您共享的内容引发任何法律纠纷或损失，由您自行承担全部责任。
  • 本软件的开发者不承担因使用本软件而产生的任何直接或间接损失。

4. 第三方服务

本软件不集成任何第三方 SDK、广告服务或统计服务。

5. 儿童隐私

本软件不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

6. 策略更新

我们可能会不时更新本隐私策略。更新后的策略将在本页面发布，恕不另行通知。

7. 联系方式

如有任何问题，请在 GitHub 提交 Issue：github.com/yxpil/PILPublisher/issues

English

Thank you for using PiLPublisher ("the Software"). We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how the Software handles your data.

1. Data Collection

This Software does not collect, store, or upload any of your personal data or files.

  • The Software does not send data to any remote server.
  • The Software does not use any analytics, tracking, or statistics tools.
  • The Software does not read files on your device that you have not explicitly added as shared folders.
  • Any access password you set is stored solely on your local device. We cannot access it.

2. File Sharing

The core functionality of the Software is to start an HTTP file server on your local area network (default port 9726), allowing other devices on the same LAN to access your chosen shared folders.

  • Shared folders and their contents are only accessible within your local network.
  • External devices on the internet cannot directly access your shared content.
  • You can set a password to restrict access.

3. Your Responsibility

The Software is a tool. You are responsible for how you use it.

  • Do not use the Software to share content that infringes on the rights of others (including but not limited to copyright, privacy, and portrait rights).
  • Do not use the Software to distribute illegal, pornographic, violent, hate speech, or otherwise harmful content.
  • You assume full responsibility for any legal disputes or losses arising from the content you share.
  • The developer of the Software assumes no liability for any direct or indirect damages resulting from the use of the Software.

4. Third-Party Services

The Software does not integrate any third-party SDKs, advertising services, or analytics services.

5. Children's Privacy

The Software is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Updated policies will be posted on this page without prior notice.
